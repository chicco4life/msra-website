<a id="studies-explained"></a>

Want to check a finding for yourself? This index links the original publications behind our [evidence review](/research/2026-08-feline-urinary-health/) and [owner guides](/learn/cats/urinary-health/). A paper's title is the authors' title; wording such as “risk reduction” does not by itself establish fewer clinical blockages. Read the population and outcome before applying a finding.

The inherited public bibliography contains references 1–40: 34 primary empirical papers and six reviews. The local working edition adds the 2025 clinical consensus guideline as reference 41. Reviews overlap the studies they discuss, and publications are not necessarily independent experiments. These are not 41 new MSRA trials.

<a id="reading-the-index"></a>

## How to read the access notes

“Relevant original full-text sections checked” means the audit examined pertinent methods, results or disclosures, rather than completing a formal assessment of the whole paper. “Indexed original sections checked” means original text was visible through an index while direct retrieval was limited. “Original abstract checked” has a narrower basis. A free full-text link is a reading route, not proof that the full paper was appraised. Notes describe MSRA's recorded access, not the availability of a paper everywhere.

No complete independent risk-of-bias appraisal or qualified clinical review of this MSRA collection has been completed. The [technical review](/research/2026-08-feline-urinary-health/) explains the missing historical methods and inclusion registries.

## Additional sources used in the owner guides

These resources provide clinical, laboratory or communication context. They were not silently added to the inherited 40-paper bibliography or the reported 46/29 review memberships. Institutional and regulator guidance is separate from empirical study evidence.

- [PDSA: blocked bladder in cats](https://www.pdsa.org.uk/pet-help-and-advice/pet-health-hub/conditions/blocked-bladder-in-cats) — UK charity owner guidance; immediate contact and care context.
- [Cornell Feline Health Center: lower urinary tract disease](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/feline-lower-urinary-tract-disease) — US university owner guidance.

- [American College of Veterinary Surgeons: urinary obstruction in male cats](https://www.acvs.org/small-animal/urinary-obstruction-in-male-cats/) — surgical-college owner resource; not an individualized surgery recommendation.
- [International Cat Care: caregiver guide to urinary tract diseases](https://icatcare.org/resources/cat-carer-guide-urinary-tract-diseases.pdf) — companion owner guidance, including practical feeding issues.
- [ISCAID urinary-tract infection guideline, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tvjl.2019.02.008) — clinical infection guidance; actual use and access are recorded in the diagnosis guide appendix.
- [Sturgess et al., 2001: urine storage and crystal detection](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11876621/) — a laboratory study using urine from 41 cats without a history of urinary disease; original abstract and inherited indexed sections, not a newly completed full-paper appraisal.

- [US FDA: meloxicam boxed warning and cats](https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/product-safety-information/information-about-boxed-warning-meloxicam-labels-regarding-safety-risks-cats) — US regulator safety context, directly read during G05 preparation.

Not a blockage-prevention indication.

## Corrections and questions

If you notice a source mismatch, tell MSRA which guide, section and publication you mean. MSRA cannot assess urgent symptoms by a research query. A cat repeatedly straining with little or no urine needs immediate veterinary contact; do not wait for a reply. [PDSA emergency guidance](https://www.pdsa.org.uk/pet-help-and-advice/pet-health-hub/conditions/blocked-bladder-in-cats)
