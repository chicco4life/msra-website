**Repeated straining with little or no urine needs immediate veterinary assessment. Contact your vet or an emergency clinic now. If you cannot tell whether urine is coming out during the attempts, say so. Do not delay care to complete this record.** [PDSA: blocked bladder](https://www.pdsa.org.uk/pet-help-and-advice/pet-health-hub/conditions/blocked-bladder-in-cats)

Copy the fields below into your phone notes or print them. Fill in what you know; use “unknown” for anything you cannot tell. Start with the contact and current-concern fields. The rest can be completed later with the clinic.

**“No urine seen” and “I watched an attempt with no urine passed” are different observations. Tell the clinic which you mean.** Do not press the abdomen or try to squeeze out urine to check.
