## Publication and scale

Guides and evidence reviews lead the hub; selected paper analyses sit below. Preserve a complete bibliography even when only a subset deserves individual analysis. New content must add a distinct owner question, interpretation, comparison or practical resource. Throughput is limited by source/editorial quality and maintenance, not an arbitrary daily count.

Use "systematic review" only for work that meets its declared methods. The current evidence anchor discloses abstract-only access and missing formal risk-of-bias grading. No volume of AI ingestion changes that status.
