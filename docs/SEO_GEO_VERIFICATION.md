## Metric interpretation

Google's historical monthly-search metric describes the term and close variants in the recorded location, network and period, **regardless of user language**. The actual language control is retained because it refines keyword ideas; it does not establish a language-filtered audience, native-speaker count, nationality or residence. Site language assignment is a reviewed content and wording choice. See Google's [historical metric definition](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en) and [keyword-idea controls](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6325025?hl=en).

These are aggregated historical keyword estimates for 1 October 2025–30 September 2026, not individual query logs. Broad UI ranges and numeric export representatives are not exact monthly counts. A range is retained only with a direct observation for that exact returned label; other ranges remain null. Close variants and overlapping country/filter observations are never added into a total. Ad competition is not organic keyword difficulty, and top-of-page bids are not actual paid CPC. All monthly_search_volume, keyword_difficulty and CPC fields stay null.
