Why we exist

Most of the pet industry builds for two animals.

98%

of US pet supplement sales go to dogs and cats.

Source: Packaged Facts, Pet Supplements in the US, 2026

The rabbit that stops eating when the season turns, the parrot that plucks itself bare, the tortoise coming out of brumation: they usually get a relabelled dog product, or nothing.

We think every animal, and every condition, deserves the same serious research, and the same honesty about what works and what is not yet proven.

MSRA-01 · ReptiliaPre-orders open

Reptile Probiotics

Tortoises · lizards · geckos · snakes

Daily digestive support, and for the times appetite dips: after brumation, a move or a change of diet. Dosed by body weight, 0.5 g per 100 g, mixed into food.

What's in it

Five live strains at ≥400 million CFU per gram, with amylase, vitamin B1 and zinc. 50 g jar.

Evidence

In a 52-week turtle trial, a multi-strain probiotic of the same class improved weight gain and shell mineralisation. That study tested a similar blend, not this product. [1]

MSRA-02 · LagomorphaLaunching next

GutDaily

Rabbits · guinea pigs · chinchillas · hamsters

Daily digestive support in a hay biscuit they eat as a treat.

What's in it

A live yeast authorised in the EU for rabbits, added after baking so it survives. The count we print is what is still alive at the end of shelf life.

Evidence

The European Food Safety Authority reassessed this strain's safety and efficacy for rabbits when renewing its authorisation in 2024. [2]

MSRA-03 · LagomorphaIn development

Settle

Rabbits · guinea pigs · chinchillas

Calming support for small mammals going through change: bonding a new companion, moving home, a heatwave.

What's in it

A calming peptide from milk protein (alpha-casozepine), and no calming herbs.

Evidence

We leave out chamomile and valerian. In published tissue studies they relax rabbit gut muscle, and a slow gut is what rabbit owners fear most. [3]

MSRA-04 · FelidaeIn development

Omega Cat

Cats

Skin and coat support, in a single-serve lickable sachet cats actually finish.

What's in it

Marine omega-3 in re-esterified triglyceride form, with the exact EPA and DHA dose printed on every sachet.

Evidence

In a 72-person double-blind trial, the re-esterified form was absorbed better than ethyl-ester fish oil. That trial was in people. [4]

MSRA-05 · AvesIn development

PlumaCalm

Parrots

A daily stick for parrots that pluck or over-preen under stress.

What's in it

Built around insoluble fibre as the lead ingredient, with a calming peptide and L-theanine.

Evidence

In a controlled study of laying hens, more insoluble fibre cut severe feather pecking from 0.78 to 0.12 pecks per 30 minutes. That evidence is from hens; parrot studies are planned before launch. [5]

MSRA-06 · FelidaeIn development

CalmCat

Cats

Calming support for cats facing a new housemate, a vet visit or changes at home, in a lickable purée they accept.

What's in it

Alpha-casozepine, a calming peptide from milk protein, with L-theanine.

Evidence

In a randomised, blinded, placebo-controlled trial, 10 of 17 cats on alpha-casozepine improved, against 4 of 17 on placebo. That trial tested the ingredient, not this product. [6]

The closed loop

Research, lab testing and manufacturing in one loop.

Many pet brands outsource both the science and the factory. We keep both inside the loop, so what the lab and the production line learn goes straight into the next formula.

  1. 01

    Research · AI-powered

    Our AI-powered research system searches and cross-checks the peer-reviewed literature, so a review that takes a traditional team months takes us days. Our team reviews every ingredient, dose and claim, and every citation traces back to the original paper.

  2. 02

    Test · in-house lab

    Our in-house research facility analyses raw materials and finished products for identity, potency and contaminants, and runs stability tests across shelf life, such as live-culture counts and fish-oil freshness.

  3. 03

    Make · in-house manufacturing

    GMP-certified production in our own facility, with runs as small as 3,000 units, so a product for a single species is possible at all. The same facility supplies fish oil and calming ingredients for one of the world's top-10 pet food brands in Asia.

  4. ↺

    Back to research

    Lab results and production data go back into the research system, so the next formula starts from a better place.
